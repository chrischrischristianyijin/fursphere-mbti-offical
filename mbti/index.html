What is MBTI?
MBTI (Myers-Briggs Type Indicator) is a personality assessment that identifies 16 personality types based on four key dichotomies: Extraversion (E) vs. Introversion (I), Sensing (S) vs. Intuition (N), Thinking (T) vs. Feeling (F), and Judging (J) vs. Perceiving (P). It helps individuals understand their strengths, decision-making styles, and interactions with others.
How does the MBTI Petsonality Test work?
This showcase lets you try the original questions about your pet’s interactions, environment, and routines. Answers stay in this browser tab. Accounts and personalized AI reports are unavailable, and no personality type is generated.
Can pets really have personality types like humans?
Pets have individual habits and preferences. FurSphere uses personality language as a playful way to explore them; this demo is not a validated psychological or veterinary assessment.
How can this test help me as a pet owner?
The questions invite you to reflect on your pet’s everyday behavior and explore the FurSphere product concept. This showcase does not provide care recommendations or an AI report.
